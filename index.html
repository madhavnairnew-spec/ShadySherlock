      About ShadySherlock

      ShadySherlock combines Extended Reality (VR, AR and MR) with predictive AI to turn forensic training and crime-scene analysis into an interactive, data-driven experience. Students step into realistic, risk-free crime scenes and practise investigative technique without contaminating real evidence.

      When evidence is damaged, fragmented or missing, predictive models use the surrounding spatial context, trajectories and structural patterns to reconstruct it and project how the events unfolded.

      How the probabilities work

      Every hypothesis starts equal. Each item of evidence carries a likelihood ratio per suspect: how much more (×>1) or less (×<1) likely that finding is if they did it. Examining evidence multiplies those ratios and renormalises (Bayes' rule), so you can trace exactly which evidence drives the result. All cases are fictional training scenarios.
