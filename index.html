    SHADY SHERLOCK

    XR FORENSIC TRAINING & PREDICTIVE RECONSTRUCTION

    ShadySherlock combines Extended Reality (VR, AR and MR) with predictive AI to turn forensic training and crime-scene analysis into an interactive, data-driven experience. Students step into risk-free virtual crime scenes and practise investigative technique without contaminating real evidence.

    When evidence is damaged, fragmented or missing, predictive models use spatial context, trajectories and structural patterns to reconstruct it and project how events unfolded.

    HOW TO INVESTIGATE

    1. Pick a case and survey the scene.
    2. Examine evidence markers. Each finding updates every suspect's probability.
    3. Run AI reconstruction to reassemble fragments, project trajectories and recover hidden findings.
    4. When confident, accuse a suspect.

    HOW PROBABILITIES WORK

    Every hypothesis starts equal. Each piece of evidence carries a likelihood ratio per suspect: how much more (×>1) or less (×<1) likely that finding is if they did it. Examining evidence multiplies these ratios and renormalises (Bayes' rule), so you can see exactly which evidence drives the result.
